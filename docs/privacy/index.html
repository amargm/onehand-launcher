Privacy Policy for One-Handed Launcher

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy describes how the One-Handed Launcher Android application ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Illusions ("the developer"). The app does not require an account, and the developer does not operate an app backend, advertising service, analytics service, or crash-reporting service.

Information the app accesses and stores on your device

  • Installed apps: As a home-screen launcher, the app asks Android for the names, icons, and package identifiers of installed apps so it can display and open them. It also keeps the package identifiers of up to five recently opened apps and app identifiers assigned to folders or widgets in the app's private storage. This information is used by the launcher on your device and is not sent by the app to the developer or to the online services described below.
  • Preferences and content you add: The app stores settings such as appearance, clock and schedule choices, folder names and contents, selected widgets, calendar country, user-created calendar events, and public-holiday results in app-private storage on your device. These items are not sent to the developer.
  • Wallpaper: When you choose a wallpaper, the image is downloaded to app-private storage and displayed by the app. The app can also ask Android to set that image as the device wallpaper.

Android's app-data backup and restore behavior may copy app-private preferences or files to a backup associated with your device or account if backup is enabled by your Android version and device settings. The developer does not receive or control such Android backups. Removing the app or clearing its storage removes its local app data, subject to any copies retained by Android backup or restore features.

Optional automatic country detection

If you tap Detect country (one-time) in the public-holiday calendar and choose to continue, Android may ask you for approximate location permission. If you grant permission, the app obtains a low-accuracy location and sends its latitude and longitude to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse-geocoding service to determine a country. The request also necessarily exposes your network address (such as your IP address) and ordinary request information to that service. This request is made to OpenStreetMap, not to the app developer.

When a country is successfully found, the app saves its two-letter country code on your device and reuses it for holiday lookups. If detection fails, you can select a country manually instead. You can decline the in-app explanation or Android permission, select a country manually, and revoke location permission later in Android settings. The app does not request location in the background.

Holiday information

When you view public holidays for a selected country and year, the app requests that country's public-holiday data from Nager.Date. If that service does not provide the requested data, the app may request it from OpenHolidays. These requests contain the selected country code, year, and normal network request information, including your network address. Results may be cached in app-private storage. Manually created calendar events remain on your device and are not sent to these services.

Wallpapers and fonts from the internet

The wallpaper picker loads thumbnail images from Unsplash and Pexels. If you preview or apply an image, the app requests the selected full-size image from the same image host. Those hosts receive the image request and ordinary network information, which may include your IP address. The wallpaper image is then stored locally as described above.

The app uses the Google Fonts for Flutter package for its typefaces and does not bundle font files in the app. When a needed font file is not already cached, the package can request it from Google's font services. Google receives the font request and ordinary network information, which may include your IP address. Google Fonts requests are made to Google, not to the app developer.

Who receives information

The developer does not receive the installed-app list, location, calendar entries, settings, or feature-use information through an app server. The app has no account system and contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting integration.

When you use the online features described above, the relevant independent service receives the data needed for that request and ordinary internet connection information such as your IP address. Specifically, location coordinates go to Nominatim; country code and year go to Nager.Date or OpenHolidays; wallpaper image requests go to Unsplash or Pexels; and uncached font requests go to Google's font services. These providers operate independently and handle request information under their own policies. The developer does not control their logging, retention, or other processing.

If you choose Contact & Support or the feedback link in the app, it opens your device's email application. The app does not send a message automatically. If you send one, the email address and message content you choose to include are delivered to the developer through your email provider and may include information you provide yourself.

Retention and choices

Settings, app identifiers used by launcher features, selected country, user-created calendar entries, cached holiday results, and a chosen wallpaper remain in app-private storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Android backup and restore may retain copies if enabled on your device. The developer does not maintain a copy of this information. Requests sent to an independent online provider may be retained by that provider under its own policy; the developer cannot access or delete those provider records on your behalf.

You can avoid sending location by choosing a country manually. Holiday lookups and online wallpapers are used only when you open or use those features. Font requests may happen automatically when the app needs a font that is not cached. Blocking the app's internet access through Android device controls prevents its network requests, but online holiday data, wallpapers, and uncached fonts will then be unavailable. You can clear app data or uninstall the app to remove its local data, and manage location permission in Android settings.

Children

The app is a general-purpose home-screen launcher and is not designed specifically for children. It does not ask for a person's name, age, or contact details. Users and guardians should review the device's location-permission and online-service choices before using the app.

Security

App preferences are stored in Android app-private storage. Requests made by the app to the online services listed above use HTTPS. No storage or network transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy is revised. The current version is available at amargm.github.io/onehand-launcher.
