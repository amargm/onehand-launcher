Privacy Policy for One-Handed Launcher

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how the One-Handed Launcher Android app ("the app") handles information. The app is a home-screen launcher. It does not require an account, and its developer does not operate a server that receives app usage data.

Information used on your device

  • Installed apps: To show launcher icons and let you open apps, the app queries Android for launchable apps and supported media apps. App names, package identifiers, and icons are used on your device and are not sent to the app developer.
  • Settings and content you create: Preferences such as appearance, folders, schedules, and calendar selections are stored in app-private storage on your device. Holiday results and the selected country code may also be cached there.
  • Widgets and wallpaper: If you add a widget, Android displays content from the widget provider you select. If you choose an online wallpaper, the image is downloaded to your device and can be applied through Android's wallpaper service.

Optional location and holiday calendar

If you use automatic country detection for holiday information, the app asks Android for approximate (coarse) location after you choose that feature. The app sends the resulting latitude and longitude to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service to determine a country. If a country is found, its two-letter country code is saved on your device. You can deny location access and select a country manually instead.

To retrieve public holidays, the app sends the selected country code and year to Nager.Date and, when needed, OpenHolidays. Holiday results may be cached on your device. These requests are made to those independent services, not to a server operated by the app developer.

Online images and fonts

The wallpaper picker loads images from Unsplash's image delivery service. The app uses Google Fonts through the google_fonts package; depending on the font cache and installed app version, font files may be requested from Google's font services. As with ordinary network requests, these services may receive your IP address and technical request information. Review their policies for details about their handling of request data.

Service providers

The app makes requests to the following independent services when you use the related features. Their handling of network request data is governed by their own terms and privacy information:

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim usage policy (country lookup)
  • Nager.Date (public holidays)
  • OpenHolidays (public holidays)
  • Unsplash privacy policy (wallpaper images)
  • Google privacy policy (font services, if requested)

Sharing, advertising, and analytics

The app developer does not sell personal information and does not operate an advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting service in the app. Information is sent to the third-party services listed above only when you use the associated network feature. The app developer does not receive the location lookup or holiday requests.

Retention and your choices

App preferences and cached holiday information remain in app-private storage until you clear the app's storage or remove the app. Android device backup settings may affect restoration of app data. You can manage or revoke location permission in Android settings, or choose a country manually. Clearing app storage removes locally stored app preferences and caches. The app developer has no account or server database from which to delete your app data. Third-party providers may retain request logs under their own policies.

Children

The app is not specifically directed to children and does not ask for a user's name, age, or contact details. Its installed-app and optional location features are described above and may be used by anyone using the device.

Security

App preferences are stored in app-private device storage. Network requests to the services above use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, service providers, or data practices change. The effective update date will be shown at the top of this page.
